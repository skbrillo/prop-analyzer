How to interpret these results

Results use a hypothetical flat $100 stake per graded selection, with profit calculated using the saved American odds.

Only selected, graded predictions contribute to win rate, profit, and ROI. Pushes are excluded from win rate. DNP and unselected predictions do not count as wagers.

Positive EV is an unvalidated model estimate, not proof of a betting edge.

Understanding the results

MAE: Mean absolute prediction error.

RMSE: Root mean squared prediction error.

Bias: Average projected minus actual value.

Historical backtests may evaluate a different model version than current live predictions. Projection accuracy does not establish profitable betting performance.
