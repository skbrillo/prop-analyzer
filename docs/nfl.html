How the picks are made — wide receivers and tight ends, receptions and receiving yards only.
  1. Hit rate: how often he went over this line in his last 10 games. The last 5 count double.
  2. Role: each past game is scaled to his current role. Projected targets = 60% his last 5 games + 40% his last 10, plus targets left by teammates ruled Out (split by the team's real WR / TE target share).
  3. Defense: shifted by how players like him do against this defense — No. 1 receivers vs No. 1 receivers, other wide receivers vs other wide receivers, tight ends vs tight ends.
  4. Coverage & blitz matchup: his production per play vs man, zone and the blitz (Rotobot), weighted by how often this defense plays man and blitzes (Sharp Football). Up to ±8%; top-50 WRs only.
  5. FantasyPros projection: counts as 5 more games, after correcting FantasyPros' usual gap to the books' lines.
  6. Market: blended with the book's no-vig price, which counts as 10 games.
  7. Pick: needs a 5+ point edge over the book. Best price across DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM and Caesars, odds −130 to +130, one pick per player, at most six per game.
Skipped: fewer than 4 projected targets, Overs in windy games, and teams starting a different QB than the one he's been catching passes from. FantasyPros / Rotobot / Sharp data switches off if the files are more than 9 days old.
Not real bets — the test is beating the closing line.
